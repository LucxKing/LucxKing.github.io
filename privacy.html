Pawscape: Pet Puzzle

Privacy Policy · Effective November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Pawscape: Pet Puzzle ("the Game", "we", "us") handles information. The Game is made by an independent developer based in California, USA.

1. Information the Game stores on your device

Your game progress (levels, coins, rescued pets, decorations, settings and similar) is saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers, we do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or location.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads, including optional rewarded videos and occasional full-screen ads between levels, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google and its partners may collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • Ad interactions (ads shown, viewed, or tapped) and diagnostic data

This information is collected by Google under its own policies. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices

  • Ad personalization: On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device Settings (Google > Ads / Privacy > Ads).
  • Consent: Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, and some U.S. states), the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown.
  • Deleting game data: Use Settings > Delete progress in the Game, or uninstall the Game. Because we do not hold your data on our servers, this removes it completely from our side.

4. California and other U.S. state privacy rights

We do not sell your personal information for money. Advertising through Google AdMob may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under some state laws. You can limit this by opting out of personalized ads as described above. Because we do not store personal information ourselves, requests to access or delete data held by Google should be made through Google.

5. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help.

6. Security

Game data stays on your device. Information collected by Google is protected under Google's security practices.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
